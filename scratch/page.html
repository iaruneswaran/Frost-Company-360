Redefining targeted
cancer therapy

TwoStep Therapeutics is developing a pipeline built around a versatile peptide that selectively binds multiple tumor-associated targets—a unique feature that enables the delivery of diverse payloads across a wide range of solid tumor types.

TwoStep’s foundational technology was developed in the labs of world renowned professors: Jennifer Cochran, Ron Levy, and Nobel Laureate Carolyn Bertozzi.
